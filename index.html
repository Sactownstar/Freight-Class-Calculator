This uses the 13-tier NMFTA density scale in effect since July 19, 2025. It is an estimate from density alone (lb per cubic foot). Your carrier’s final class comes from the NMFC item number for what you’re shipping, and some commodities have a fixed class or special stowability rules. Confirm with your carrier before booking. Linear feet is total floor footprint divided by 8 ft of trailer width; it ignores stacking and loading patterns, so carriers may count it differently.
